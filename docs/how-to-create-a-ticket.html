Narration script (for recording a voiceover / real screencast)
  1. Intro: “In this short walkthrough, you'll learn how to create a support ticket in Anchor — and how to get help even faster.”
  2. Open the portal: “From the left sidebar, open ‘Get help’ to reach the support portal.”
  3. Ask Anchor first: “Before filing a ticket, try ‘Ask Anchor.’ It answers your question instantly from the knowledge base. If that resolves your issue, you're done — no ticket needed.”
  4. Submit a request: “If you still need help, click ‘Submit a request’ and choose the type that fits: something's broken, you need something, access or account help, or a question.”
  5. Describe it: “Give a clear subject, then describe what happened, what you expected, and any error messages. Important — never attach sensitive data: no CUI, passwords, or raw log files. Redact first.”
  6. Set priority: “Set the impact and urgency. Anchor derives the priority automatically.”
  7. Submit & track: “Submit. You'll get a ticket number, email updates, and can track progress under ‘My requests.’”
  8. Outro: “That's it. For a P1 emergency after hours, call the NexusCyber Hotline at (800) 265-6446.”

Tip: to produce a real video, screen-record the live portal at anchor.azurewebsites.us following these beats, and read this script as the voiceover.
