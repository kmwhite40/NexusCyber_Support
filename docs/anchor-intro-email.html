SubjectIntroducing Anchor — production-ready and rolling out to our Carbon customers

Leadership,

I'm pleased to introduce Anchor, our purpose-built platform for running IT service management and security operations in the government cloud. Anchor is live at anchor.azurewebsites.us, and we will be using it to serve our Carbon customers.

In one sentence: Anchor gives SBS a single place to take in requests, resolve incidents, fulfill access, monitor security posture, and produce compliance evidence — for many government customers at once, with strict separation between them. Think of it as a ServiceNow-class service desk that was born inside the FedRAMP / CUI boundary rather than retrofitted into it.

Why this matters for SBS
  • Lower cost, full control — ServiceNow-class capability without the licensing and lock-in; we own the roadmap.
  • Compliance by design — built in the gov boundary, so audit evidence is a by-product of operations, not a fire drill.
  • One system of record — service desk and security in one place; fewer tools, cleaner data.
  • Scales as an MSP — each new customer onboards as an isolated tenant, all run from one platform.
  • Meets users where they are — Microsoft 365, email, and Entra ID SSO mean minimal change management.
Production Readiness

All quality checks have been conducted by our team and interns, and the platform is ready for production. We are confident in onboarding our Carbon customers onto Anchor.

Near-term, Product is investing in deeper cloud provisioning (M365, Azure Gov, AWS GovCloud straight from the catalog), broader automation, and richer executive insight — continuing to close the gap with premium commercial tools while staying purpose-built for our mission.

I've attached a short feature one-pager and an executive briefing deck for reference. I'd welcome the chance to give leadership a 20-minute walkthrough — a quick tour of the platform and a look at the roadmap. Happy to find time that works.
