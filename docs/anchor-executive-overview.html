The one-linerWhat is Anchor?

Anchor is our purpose-built platform for running IT service management and security operations in the government cloud. It gives SBS a single place to take in requests, resolve incidents, fulfill access, monitor security posture, and produce compliance evidence — for many government customers at once, with strict separation between them. Think of it as a ServiceNow-class service desk that was born in the FedRAMP / CUI boundary rather than retrofitted into it.

What it doesCapabilities at a glance

IT Service Management

Modern ticketing with priorities and SLAs, agent queues, on-call paging, and full incident, change, and problem management — the day-to-day engine of the service desk.

Self-Service & Automation

A service catalog with guided request forms (access, provisioning, cloud), approval workflows, a knowledge base, and “Ask Anchor” — an assistant that deflects routine questions and speeds fulfillment.

Security Posture & Compliance

Continuous monitoring, security-posture scoring, an asset inventory, and an immutable audit trail — turning everyday operations into audit-ready evidence mapped to NIST 800-53 / FedRAMP controls.

Microsoft 365 & Cloud

Native Microsoft 365 (GCC) email notifications and email-to-ticket, Entra ID single sign-on, and Teams — meeting customers where they already work, plus catalog support for Azure Gov and AWS GovCloud requests.

Multi-Tenant Trust

One platform, many customers, with tenant isolation enforced at the database, least-privilege roles, just-in-time elevation with break-glass, and delegated org-scoped administration.

Insight & Reporting

Executive and operational dashboards, SLA and workload analytics, and self-service reporting — so leadership can see service health and customers can see the value being delivered.

The business caseWhy Anchor matters

What it changes for SBS and our customers

  • Lower cost, full control. ServiceNow-class capability without the licensing and lock-in — we own the roadmap.
  • Compliance by design. Built in the gov boundary, so audit evidence is a by-product of operations, not a fire drill.
  • One system of record. Service desk and security in one place — fewer tools, less swivel-chair, cleaner data.
  • Scales as an MSP. Onboard a new customer as an isolated tenant; run them all from one platform.
  • Meets users where they are. Microsoft 365, email, and SSO — little change management for end users.
  • Faster resolution. Automation, catalog, and self-service deflect routine work and speed fulfillment.

How it's deliveredBuilt for the boundary

Anchor runs natively in Azure Government at anchor.azurewebsites.us, keeping CUI workloads inside the authorization boundary. Access is governed end-to-end — Entra ID single sign-on, role-based permissions, and isolation enforced all the way down to the database — so a customer only ever sees their own data, and platform administration is delegated safely. Updates ship continuously and apply in-boundary, so customers get new capability without disruptive upgrades.

At a glance

  • Hosting: Azure Government
  • Identity: Microsoft Entra ID SSO
  • Isolation: Per-tenant, DB-enforced
  • Compliance: NIST 800-53 / FedRAMP-aligned
  • Model: Multi-tenant MSP
