One platform to take in requests, resolve incidents, fulfill access, and prove compliance.

Anchor is our purpose-built platform for IT service management and security operations in the government cloud — serving many customers at once with strict separation between them. Think of it as a service desk that was born in the FedRAMP / CUI boundary rather than retrofitted into it.

01

IT Service Management

Executive and operational dashboards, SLA and workload analytics, and self-service reporting — so leadership sees service health at a glance.

How it's delivered

Built for the boundary

Anchor runs natively in Azure Government, keeping CUI workloads inside the authorization boundary. Access is governed end-to-end — Entra ID single sign-on, role-based permissions, and isolation enforced all the way down to the database — so a customer only ever sees their own data, and platform administration is delegated safely. Updates ship continuously and apply in-boundary, so customers get new capability without disruptive upgrades.
